
# Required trailers for a meaningful commit:
Evolution-Type: done
Evolution-Refs: auto
Evolution-Expected: describe the observable result
Evolution-Why: describe why this change is needed
Evolution-Rollout: none — explain why no rollout is needed
Evolution-Rollback: describe how to recover or explain why not applicable
Evolution-Next: owner — concrete action — review date

# For Evolution-Type: fixed
# Evolution-Root-Cause: describe the confirmed root cause

# For Evolution-Type: decision
# Evolution-Choice: describe the selected choice
